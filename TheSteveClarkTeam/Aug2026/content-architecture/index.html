Priority 0 · Critical Fixes (Before New Content)

FIX 1
18 community pages average 68 original words each, 1,218 words across the whole footprint. Rebuild as Tier 2 guides.
FIX 2
IDX template serves 87 F.C. Tucker listing cards against 38 Compass on the team's own domain. Rebalance the template.
FIX 3
Zero FAQPage schema site-wide. Single highest-leverage AEO gap, and no competitor in the market has it either.
FIX 4
Blog holds one post since July 2020, titled "placeholder blog post title." Remove it and start the real cadence.
FIX 5
Zero data tables site-wide. Engines cite pages that carry original dated numbers, and there are none.
FIX 6
One H2 per community page. No heading structure for passage retrieval or answer extraction.
FIX 7
Production figures conflict: site says $115M over two years and 1,200+ families, Compass says $500M+ career and 1,500+.
FIX 8
The agent bio pages carry no Person schema and no sameAs graph. Named experts are what engines cite.
FIX 9
Williams Creek page pulls general 46240 inventory from $229K to $579K, not Williams Creek comparables. Tighten the IDX radius.

Open Questions · Needed to Unblock the Build

The intake questionnaire was never returned. Everything above was assembled from the public record. These eight answers finalize it.
Q1 · Blocks the entity layer
Which production numbers publish everywhere? Engines cross-check, and the site and Compass profile currently disagree.
Q2 · Blocks nine author pages
Who owns which lane, who can be quoted, and who stays out of public content?
Q3 · Sets pillar weighting
Buyer or seller weighted, and which price bands lead the community guides?
Q4 · Sizes the differentiator
How much of the business is new construction, how direct can we be, and which builder relationships are off limits?
Q5 · Sequences 18 town guides
Which communities are primary, secondary or IDX only? Our working split is shown in Tier 2 above.
Q6 · Defines Tier 3
Which 20 to 25 subdivisions carry the most volume and the most of your business?
Q7 · In or out
Compass Concierge, bridge loans and Private Exclusives as content? We would cover cost and tradeoffs honestly.
Q8 · Blocks publishing entirely
Who controls WordPress, GA4, Search Console and the IDX template? Fix 2 needs whoever built the site.
