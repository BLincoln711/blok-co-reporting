01 · The finding

Your name is solved. The question that finds you is not.

Across identical markets, identical days and identical engines, an agent's own website is cited on 84 percent of branded searches and 15 percent of the searches a stranger actually uses to pick an agent. That single contrast is the finding. A team that checks its own name concludes AI search is handled. The question that acquires a customer is the one they are absent from.

Of the seven "best team in this market" questions tracked, five returned the agent's own site zero times out of eleven or twelve attempts.

The two that won returned it on seven of ten and four of six. Both are markets where a full content architecture had already been published. That is the most commercially useful line in this dataset. The selection question is not unwinnable. It is unwinnable without the asset.

03 · The engine split

These are not one market. They are three separate source systems.

Only 5.8 percent of the domains in this edition were cited by all three engines. YouTube appears in 38 percent of Google AI Overview answers and is effectively absent from ChatGPT. Reddit runs 39 percent on Perplexity and 6 percent on ChatGPT. Any product reporting a single blended AI visibility score is measuring one system and calling it the market.

05 · What this edition cannot claim

The limits are published because an index that hides them is not an index.

Every figure above is a direct reading, not a model, an estimate or a number borrowed from someone else's study. That also means the boundaries are hard, and they are worth stating before anyone quotes a figure from this page.

Read at national level material

Readings were taken at United States national level rather than from inside each market. A question with local intent asked from a national vantage point can return a different answer than the same question asked in market. Treat every market-level rate here as a floor rather than a reading of what a local buyer sees.

Credit, not mention

This edition records whether an engine credited a source. It does not record whether an engine said a brand name aloud without linking to it. Those are two different events, and only the first one is measured here.

One week, not a quarter

Every figure comes from a single seven day window, 2026-08-18 to 2026-08-25. This is one reading of a system that moves. No trend, direction or month over month claim is made anywhere in this edition.

Unequal sampling across engines

Perplexity contributes 850 answers, ChatGPT 444 and Google 304. Pooled figures therefore lean toward Perplexity. The per-engine numbers, which carry their own denominators, are the safer read for any engine-to-engine comparison.

The Google reading is the thinnest

Google's AI Overview is the least stable of the three surfaces to read at scale, and the suppression finding rests on the smallest sample. It is reported because it is actionable and flagged here because it is thin.

Not a demand-weighted sample

The 156 questions were chosen for twelve specific markets, and they are phrased more tightly than the longer conversational wording people actually use with an assistant. Nothing here says how often a real buyer asks any of them. Search demand weighting is absent entirely.

Presence, not prominence

A citation is recorded as present or absent. Whether it led the answer or sat fifteenth in a long source panel, and whether the mention was favourable, are not captured. A single inline credit and one entry in a long list count identically.

Three engines

This edition covers Perplexity, ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. Other assistants were not part of this reading, and no figure, ranking or prediction is offered for them.
